# PTS design delivery rule

Read frontend/AGENTS.md and docs/15-design-delivery/00-START-HERE.md for all visible UI tasks. Before making code, consult the selected issue/design direction and the existing PTS public-ui-ux-design-system.md.

Do not use generic all-caps eyebrows, gratuitous 01/02 steps, repeated card stacks, invented solar data or AI artwork as evidence of real customer projects. A design task must end with the actual rendered desktop/mobile result, screenshots after scroll and independent review. Do not describe CSS/SVG 2.5D as WebGL 3D.

Root AGENTS.md remains authoritative for safety and production. Creative deviations need explicit scope/owner approval and functional/data/SEO preservation.
